# Design direction: Lagos daylight (2026-10-03)

Replaces the Warm Editorial look. Chosen by the project owner after the impeccable and taste skills flagged cream + serif + terracotta as the category default. Surfaces: Market (Operate), Double-claim test (Operate), About & impact (Read). Code-led build: the impeccable launcher was not run (it downloads an unaudited binary), so there is no concept-seed key or comp; its reference playbooks were followed directly.

## Direction contract

**THESIS.** Sunpool reads like a Lagos street in daylight: one colour, sun yellow, and it marks only what is solar energy or money changing hands. It refuses the editorial newspaper (cream, serif, terracotta, tracked labels) and the dark fintech terminal.

**OWN-WORLD.** Cool off-white ground `#F7F7F4`, near-black ink `#111214`, sun yellow `#F2B705` used only as a filled area (never as text or a thin line on the light ground, 1.69:1), with ink on top (10.3:1). Hairlines are ink at 12%. One radius scale: 8px controls, 12px panels, full pills only for status badges. No offset shadows; elevation is a border, and floating layers alone get a soft shadow. Bricolage Grotesque for headings, Geist for UI and body, JetBrains Mono for kWh, prices, addresses and hashes. Lucide icons.

**STORY.** A neighbour opens the app and sees energy moving right now (totals and the live tape), understands every kWh is backed by a reading that can be claimed once, and then buys or lists in one step.

**FIRST VIEWPORT.** A 64px header. Below it, a full-width sun band: the headline on the left, the three totals set large in ink mono on the yellow field, and the primary action (List surplus, an ink button) on the band. Under the band, the generation chart (8 of 12 columns) beside the trade tape (4 of 12).

**FORM.** Danfo and street-signage colour discipline (yellow and black, plain grotesk lettering), carried as palette and type only. It never supplies layout or controls: the screen stays a standard product layout with standard controls. First on the candidate list.

**SIGNATURE MOVE.** Sun fill: anything that is solar energy is drawn as a yellow filled area. The metered part of the generation curve is a yellow area under an ink line; the totals sit on the yellow band; a newly settled trade flashes a yellow wash that fades out.

**FINISH.** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
