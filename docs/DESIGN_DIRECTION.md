# Design direction: Deep forest (2026-10-03)

Replaces Lagos daylight (same day), which replaced Warm Editorial. The owner's feedback: the site read like a newspaper, with too much text and detail, and it was not expressive. They also asked for a darker palette. Three dark options were mocked up (Night sun, Deep forest, Midnight amber) and the owner picked **Deep forest**, along with the "prepaid power" framing.

**THESIS.** Sunpool should feel like a phone app you already know how to use: top up, done. One big number, Buy or Sell, a few neighbours, one lime button.

**WORLD.** Dark forest green ground, mist text, lime as the single accent, Bricolage Grotesque extra bold headlines, JetBrains Mono numbers. Tokens and contrast are in `DESIGN.md`.

**STORY.** A shop owner in Surulere opens Sunpool at noon. They see 14 kWh of solar for sale on their street, tap Balogun two doors away, and load 5 kWh. The smart meter on the shared line counts it down. Balogun is paid instantly, and the reading can never be sold twice.

**CONCEPT.** Prepaid power from your neighbour's roof. DisCo prepaid units are locked to a meter and are never resold. Sunpool sells solar surplus over a shared line with its own smart meter, which is simulated in the demo.

**FIRST VIEWPORT.** Headline with one lime phrase, three small totals, and the trade panel beside it (below it on phones).

**SIGNATURE MOVE.** Solar is always a lime filled area: the sun curve on Market, the metered area on Activity, the cost bars on About.
